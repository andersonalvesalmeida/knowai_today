Anderson Alves

The code side of AI

Anderson has been writing software for more than 20 years. In 2007, when almost nobody in Brazil was talking about artificial intelligence, he already had published research and a working system: ParaCred, which assessed bank credit risk using AI and paraconsistent logic, able to make decisions even when faced with contradictory information. The project went from Unisanta university to the Santos Business Incubator.

He worked at tech companies such as DClick, Eicon, Planetun and Inpulso before building, together with Bruno in 2018, the WORLD's first AI content creation system: Manycontent.
